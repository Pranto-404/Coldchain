Task 3
Door left open
Fridge Alpha's door opens. After 60 s the buzzer sounds and an alert goes out by Telegram/email if configured.

            
Task 3
Power failure
Mains cut on Fridge Alpha. Compressor stops, battery drains, status shows battery.

            
Task 3
Wi-Fi outage
Readings queue on the node and sync in order, without duplicates, when Wi-Fi returns.

            
Task 5
Weak compressor
Cooling capacity falls ~70 %. The health score drops on the next cooling run.

            
Task 5
Rising risk
Alpha warms under load with a weak compressor; the forecast flags it and suggests moving stock to Beta.

Heat budget
Put a labelled sample batch at a known exposure and check its status.
